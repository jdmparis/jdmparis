# Hi, I'm Julie 👋

I work in AI by day and build my own agents by night. This page is the night part. I'm an economist by training, not a computer scientist. That's why I build: I want to understand AI from the keyboard, not just the boardroom.

* 🔨 **I build AI agents and micro tools to make my life run better.** Some are public, others not (yet):

  * 🕵️‍♀️ **Rachel**: my intelligence analyst. She listens to the investor calls nobody has time for — 50+ retail, consumer, and luxury companies — and distills what the C-suite is really talking about. Her job: keep me the most knowledgeable person in the room.

  * 🧭 **Josh**: helps my family and me navigate moves across countries and the long game of my career.

* 🕸️ **These aren't four agents. They're four swarms.** Each orchestrates its own subagents; they share memory and coordinate through a sprint board where they leave each other notes. Built with Claude Code and custom MCP servers: apps on Vercel and Cloudflare, data in Supabase and Upstash, shared brain in Obsidian. It's the orchestration problem every enterprise is about to face, in miniature.
